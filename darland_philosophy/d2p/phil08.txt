Now a first question is what is there?

I am inclined to say events. Both Russell and Whitehead said this at times.

I also believe there are universals (including relations). Sometimes these are called predicates, but I prefer the term "universal", as, to me, "predicate", 
has linguistic connotations.

I would think all events would be of lowest PM type and there is a hierarchy of higher types. I am also inclined to believe there are only a finite number 
of entities in any type.

wouldn't the same question arise? What about fx =df (x = x)?
